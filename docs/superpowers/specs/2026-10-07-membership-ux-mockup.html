Proposal · payments UX

Padmakara Membership Flow

Every screen a member meets, from a locked retreat to cancelling, laid out in order. Each step reuses a pattern people already know from sites they pay every month, so nothing here needs explaining.

Everywhere in the app we say membership and contribution, never "subscription". It suits the spirit of a dharma community, and it is what Apple requires of the iPhone app anyway.

Open decisions this mockup assumes

  • €5 a month minimum, with €10 and €20 suggested and any amount above allowed.
  • Yearly at the same price, twelve months in one payment, no discount, as André asked.
  • Card and Direct Debit for monthly. Yearly adds MB WAY and Multibanco, which only work for one-off payments.
  • The €15 one-off retreat purchase and the 5 € / 10 € tiers are not drawn. They fit in later without changing these screens.
A · Meeting the paywall

A locked retreat says what it is and how to get in

Today the lock banner leads to Settings, which has no way to pay. The web lock leads straight to the contribution page. The iPhone app may not show a price or a pay button, so it states the fact plainly and stops there.

B · Joining

Choose, pay, and know where you stand at every moment

Four screens, the same rhythm as the Guardian or Wikipedia contribution pages: pick an amount, see a plain summary, pay inside a page that still feels like Padmakara, then a clear result. The amounts and the toggle on screen 3 are live, click them.

B′ · When it doesn't go straight through

Every outcome gets its own clear screen

These replace two misleading pages we have today: "Membership cancelled" shown to someone who only closed the form, and "activated" shown before any payment arrived.

C · Managing the membership

One account page with everything in view

Status, amount, next payment, payment method and history on one page, actions listed underneath, the way Spotify, Netflix and every bank app lay it out. Today the button labelled "Manage" cancels the membership in one tap with no warning. That goes away.

D · Emails

Four messages, each sent by us

Easypay sends nothing to the member on a successful payment, so all of these come from Padmakara, in the member's language.

Padmakara · right after joiningWelcome, your membership is active

Amount and frequency, next payment date, a link to the retreats, and how to cancel. Required by the card schemes for recurring payments.

Padmakara · yearly members only, 7 days beforeYour yearly membership renews on 7 Oct

Amount, date, and a link to change or cancel. Expected for long renewal periods.

Padmakara · when a renewal failsWe couldn't take your payment

Access continues until the grace date, with one button to update the payment method.

Padmakara · after cancellingYour membership ends on 7 Nov

Confirms the end date and offers to resume with one click.
