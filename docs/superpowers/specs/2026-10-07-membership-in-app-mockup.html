Membership inside the app

Today joining leaves the app twice. The membership screens sit outside the app's frame, with no navigation, no brand panel and no way back. Then payment jumps to a separate page on api.padmakara.pt. Both pages also speak a different visual language: rounded pills, an iOS-style toggle, a pink rounded box and a system-font button. The rest of the app uses small-caps titles, hairline lists and square burgundy buttons.

Recommendation: keep the whole journey inside the app's frame, in the app's own vocabulary, and embed Easypay's form in our page instead of sending people to a separate one. The card fields stay Easypay's, as they must for security, but they sit inside a Padmakara screen, tinted with our colours.

Is a separate payment page acceptable?

It works, but it is the weaker of three options

Today Separate page, our own design

A page on api.padmakara.pt that loads Easypay's form. People trust it well enough, but they lose the app around them, there is no navigation, and its look matches neither the app nor Easypay.

Common, acceptable Easypay's own hosted page

Like Stripe Checkout or PayPal: an obvious hand-off to the payment company. Familiar and trusted, but entirely Easypay-branded. It suits shops more than a community that already has a warm, coherent app.

Recommended Embedded in an app screen

Easypay's form is designed to be embedded: its "inline" mode mounts in any element of our page. The member never leaves Padmakara, back always works, and the player keeps going. Only the card fields come from Easypay.

What it takes

Two pieces of work, in this order

StepWhatEffortRisk
1. Into the frame, in the app's styleMove the membership screens into the app's tab group (like Settings). Restyle them with the existing pieces: small-caps titles, section labels, hairline rows, the square button. Membership becomes a row under Settings.About one sessionLow: screens and styles only. The payment logic and the tests stay.
2. Embed Easypay's formA web-only payment screen that mounts Easypay's form inline with our colours, replacing the separate page on the API server. Redirects after payment become in-app navigation.About one session, plus a sandbox runMedium: needs checking that 3-D Secure and Direct Debit behave inline. If anything misbehaves, the current page stays available as a fallback.

The fixes you asked for today (a back link, a clearer "other amount" field, € after the number) are being made now on the current screens, and step 1 carries them forward.
